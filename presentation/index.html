Problem

Night messages do not wait for morning staff

Fashion shops on Instagram and Messenger lose sales when nobody is online.

Delayed replies

Customers ask about size, price, and stock after hours. A slow answer often ends the sale.

Unreliable answers

Staff quote outdated prices or invent delivery promises when the catalog is not at hand.

Handoff chaos

When a buyer is ready, context is scattered across chats, notes, and memory.

No operating picture

Owners cannot see whether AI or humans are closing the loop.

Solution

A sales workspace that answers from the catalog

AI handles the first reply. Humans confirm the order.

01

Load the shop

Catalog, variants, stock, and rules become the only source of truth.

02

Talk like the shop

Customers write in French, Arabic, or Derja. The AI matches the register.

03

Stay grounded

Prices, stock, and delivery come from tools — never from invention.

04

Hand off to buy

Purchase intent becomes a pending order with a briefing for staff.

AI behaviour

Derja-first, catalog-bound

The model is allowed to speak. It is not allowed to invent the shop.

Language

French, MSA, Tunisian Arabic, and Arabizi. Demo prompts use Derja such as “slm, andkom pntallon blue”.

Grounding

Stock, price, and policy answers come from tools. Golden-set gates block unsafe commercial claims.

Handoff

Human-required triggers pause AI and open a briefing so staff continue without re-asking.

Playground

Merchants rehearse replies against a sandboxed store before going live.

# Phase 4 — Payments and sales automation

**Blocked.** Do not split into cards until Phase 3 exit (or the sanctioned `04` §10 payments-forward ADR) and Phase 4 specs exist. Do not invent Stripe, D17, Flouci, Konnect, or payment-link `T-*` here.

## Goal

Reduce human workload per order while keeping confirmation trust (`04` §7.1).

## Headline capability

Payment links, `auto_send` default for mature tenants, lifecycle automation, advanced analytics (`04` §3).

## Exit signal

Human messages per confirmed order down; CSAT stable (`04` §3 / §7.5).

## Entry

Phase 3 exit, **or** a recorded ADR if Phase 1 conversion is strong and merchants demand payments (`04` §10). AI still never approves refunds (`04` §7.4).

## Cards

None until unblocked.
